- 20 human types (civilians, soldiers, riot officers, knights, zombies that infect, skeletons, androids, combat droids, aliens, giants, gnomes, ninjas...) plus a character editor
- 31 firearms and energy weapons: pistols, SMGs, rifles, snipers, shotguns, LMG, minigun, rocket & grenade launchers, flamethrower, laser, railgun, tesla gun, cryo ray, gravity gun, medi beam, crossbow, nail gun, flare gun, plasma cannon, water blaster
- 16 melee weapons including a working chainsaw
- 12 explosives from grenades to a tactical nuke
- 8 vehicles, thrusters, pistons, fans, magnets, conveyors, launch pads, gravity fields
- Electrical system: batteries, buttons, switches, timers, sensors, tripwires, pressure plates, NOT gates, lamps, sirens, shock pads, tesla coils
- Traps: saw blades, spike traps, bear traps, burners, sentry turrets
- Tools: drag, use, rope, spring, weld, pin, wire, build, fire, blast, cut, heal, delete
- 10 maps, slow motion, gravity control, autosave, save manager with thumbnails, file export/import
